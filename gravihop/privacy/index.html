GraviHop Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

GraviHop ("the app") is published by EKELAB CORP, Florida, USA ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or receive any personal information ourselves. The game has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your scores, stars, unlocked items, daily-challenge history and settings are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them (Android may restore them from your own Google device backup).

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app can show an optional rewarded video ad when you choose "Watch an ad to continue". Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • general device and app information (model, OS version, language, approximate location derived from IP address),
  • ad interactions and app diagnostics.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent tool before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

"GraviHop Premium" is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play that you own Premium.

Children

GraviHop is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell or share personal information. The only third parties that process data are Google (AdMob and Google Play), as described above.

Security

Data processed by Google's services is encrypted in transit.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date.
