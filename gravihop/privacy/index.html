GraviHop

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

GraviHop ("the app") is published by EKELAB CORP, Florida, USA ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Information we collect

The game has no accounts and no servers of its own, and we do not collect your name, email address or contacts. Your scores, stars, unlocked items, daily-challenge history and settings are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them (Android may restore them from your own Google device backup).

The only information that reaches us is anonymous crash and stability data, described below.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics, a Google service, so we can find and fix problems.

Each time the app starts, it sends a small session record (the installation identifier described below, device model, OS version and app version). This is what lets us see what share of sessions end in a crash. It does not include what you do in the game.

When the app crashes or hits an internal error, it also sends a report containing:

  • what went wrong and where in the app's code,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, app version, language, free memory and storage, screen orientation),
  • a random installation identifier created by the app, used to count how many devices a problem affects. It is not your advertising ID and is not linked to your name or account.

This data is used only to keep the app working, is not used for advertising, and is deleted by Google after 90 days.

How Firebase handles this data: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app can show an optional rewarded video ad when you choose "Watch an ad to continue". Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • general device and app information (model, OS version, language, approximate location derived from IP address),
  • ad interactions and app diagnostics.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent tool before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

"GraviHop Premium" is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play that you own Premium.

Children

GraviHop is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell or share personal information. The only third party that processes data is Google (AdMob, Firebase Crashlytics and Google Play), as described above.

Security

Data processed by Google's services is encrypted in transit.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date.
